Progression

  • Start with a weight you can do for the bottom of the rep range.
  • Add reps until every set reaches the top of the range.
  • Then add a little weight and start again at the bottom. The app tells you when.

Effort

  • Stop most sets with about 1–2 reps left.
  • Exercises tagged last set to failure can go all the way on the final set.
  • Warm up: 2 light sets of the first exercise, plus light laterals or face pulls.

Shoulder

  • Skip the plate-loaded reverse-grip pulldown machine. It's the one that clicks.
  • Clicking without pain is fine. If something hurts, swap it, and get it checked if it continues.
  • Ask the fitness specialist to spot heavy bench sets, or use the safety bars.

Is it working?

  • Most lifts going up every 1–2 weeks.
  • Weekly average body weight rising about 0.15–0.3 kg.
  • Weight flat for 2 weeks: eat a bit more. Gaining much faster: a bit less.
